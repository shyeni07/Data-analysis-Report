# Financial Analytics Dashboard

An interactive financial analytics dashboard built using **Google Data Studio (Looker Studio) and Microsoft Excel** to analyze financial performance, revenue, profitability, budget vs actual performance, and receivables.

## 📊 Project Overview

Project Link:- https://datastudio.google.com/reporting/25eaeba2-6780-4771-a501-782f1b0291c6

This project focuses on transforming financial data into an interactive dashboard that provides a clear view of business performance.

The dashboard consists of three main sections:

1. **Financial Overview** – Analyzes revenue, direct costs, gross profit, monthly revenue trends, budget vs actual revenue, service-line contribution, industry contribution, and client contribution.
2. **Revenue & Profitability** – Analyzes gross margin, client revenue vs gross profit, top clients by gross profit, and profitability by industry.
3. **Receivables** – Tracks paid, pending, and overdue revenue along with client-level payment status.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The dashboard was designed to answer key financial questions:

- How much revenue did the business generate?
- How does actual revenue compare with budgeted revenue?
- Which service lines generate the most revenue and profit?
- How is revenue distributed across industries and clients?
- Which clients contribute the most gross profit?
- How much revenue has been paid, is pending, or is overdue?
- Where are the major financial performance gaps?

## 🔍 Key Insights

### Revenue Performance

The dashboard shows total revenue of **₹12.73M**, with **₹5.75M** in direct costs and **₹6.98M** in gross profit. The resulting gross margin is approximately **54.83%**.

### Budget vs Actual Revenue

The dashboard compares the company's estimated budgeted revenue with the actual revenue generated.

Across the months shown, actual revenue remains significantly below the budgeted revenue, highlighting a persistent gap between planned and realized revenue.

### Industry Contribution

Healthcare is the largest individual industry contributor with approximately **₹2.06M** in revenue.

Engineering, Hospitality, and Retail contribute approximately **₹1.31M, ₹1.28M, and ₹1.21M** respectively.

The remaining industries are grouped under **Others**, contributing approximately **₹6.88M**.

### Service-Line Performance

Revenue and gross profit are analyzed across service lines including:

- Bookkeeping
- Tax & Compliance
- Accounting
- Payroll
- Financial Reporting
- Paraplanning

This helps compare revenue contribution with profitability across different services.

### Receivables

The receivables section provides visibility into payment collection:

- **78.8% Paid**
- **16.7% Pending**
- **4.5% Overdue**

The dashboard also provides client-level visibility into pending and overdue invoices.
